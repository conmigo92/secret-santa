How to play

Christmas Trivia. One phone for the room. Read a question and keep score.

What you need

One phone everyone can see, and at least one player or team. Nobody needs an account.

Setup

Add a name for each player or team. Set the seconds for each question, and set how many questions to play. You can add your own questions before you start. Those questions are always in the game, so the count has to be at least that many.

A turn

The page shows a question and four choices. The timer counts down. A timer of 0 waits until you show the answer. Then give one point to one player, or give no point, and go to the next question.

How it ends

After the last question, the page lists the scores. You can also end early and keep the points you have.

Tips

  • The game stays in this browser until someone starts a new one.
  • A custom question needs four different choices and one correct answer.
