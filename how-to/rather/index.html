How to play

Would You Rather. One phone, two choices, and a count of hands.

What you need

People who can raise a hand, and one phone. Nobody needs an account.

Setup

Open the page. The prompts are already shuffled. Shuffle again if you have played this pile.

A turn

Read both sides. Tap a side once for each hand, or tap Minus if you counted one too many. The tally cannot go below zero. Next clears the count and shows another prompt.

How it ends

After the last prompt, the page says the pile is done. Shuffle again for a new order.

Tips

  • The tally is only for the prompt on screen.
  • The place in the pile stays in this browser.
