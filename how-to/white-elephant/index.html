How to play

White Elephant. One phone for the room. Open a gift, or steal one.

What you need

Two or more players, one wrapped gift each, and one phone everyone can see.

Setup

Add every name. The page shuffles the turn order. A gift locks after 3 steals. Set the steal limit to 0 and no one can steal. Player 1, first in that order, gets one final swap. By default you can't steal a gift straight back. The host can change the steal limit, the steal-back switch, and the final swap on the setup screen.

A turn

Open a wrapped gift and type what was inside, or steal an open gift. A locked gift stays put. If you steal, the person who lost it goes next.

How it ends

When every player has opened one gift, Player 1 may trade once or keep their gift. That trade ends the game. If the host turned the final swap off, the game ends when the last gift is opened.

Tips

  • A locked gift stays where it is, including on the final swap.
  • The game stays in this browser until someone starts a new one.
