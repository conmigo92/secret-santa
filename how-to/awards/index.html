How to play

Most Likely To. Pass one phone. Hand out the titles at the end.

What you need

Two or more people, and one phone. Nobody needs an account.

Setup

The host adds every name and sets how many awards to give, from 1 to 12. The page picks that many prompts.

A turn

Pass the phone. The screen names the next voter. They tap one person for that prompt, including themselves if they want. Then hand the phone on. Do this for every person, then the next prompt.

How it ends

After the last vote, each person gets the titles they won. A tie gives the title to everyone who tied. A person with no title is listed too.

Tips

  • The titles stay hidden while you are still voting.
  • The awards stay in this browser until someone starts new ones.
