How to play

Ugly Sweater. Pass one phone. Vote, then reveal.

What you need

Two or more people in sweaters, and one phone. Nobody needs an account.

Setup

The host adds every contestant. Those same people vote.

A turn

Pass the phone. The screen names the next voter and hides the last ballot. That person picks someone else for ugliest, most creative, and most festive. You can't vote for yourself. Then hand the phone on.

How it ends

When everyone has voted, the totals stay hidden. The host taps Reveal winners. A tie lists every name.

Tips

  • Look away while the phone is with someone else.
  • The contest stays in this browser until someone starts a new one.
