How to play

Holiday Bingo. Each player gets a different card of things that happen at the party.

What you need

One phone or a printer for each card. Nobody needs an account.

Setup

The host taps New card once per player. Each link holds that card. Send a different link to each person, or open it and print it.

A turn

The center square is free. Tap a square when that moment happens. Five in a row, down, or on a diagonal is bingo. The page says Bingo when you have one.

How it ends

The first person to call bingo wins, or play until a time you pick. This page does not pick a winner for the room.

Tips

  • The same link always opens the same card.
  • Marks stay in that browser until you clear the site data.
