How to play

Charades or Pictionary. One phone, one word, and a team that guesses.

What you need

Two or more teams, and one phone. For Pictionary, bring paper and a pencil. Nobody needs an account.

Setup

Add each team. Pick charades or pictionary, and pick easy, medium, hard, or any mix. Set the seconds for a turn.

A turn

The guessers look away. The actor taps Show word. In charades, act the word with no talking. In pictionary, draw it with no letters and no talking. Got it gives that team a point and passes the phone. Skip brings a new word for the same team. Next team passes with no point.

How it ends

When the word pile is empty, the page lists the scores. You can also end early.

Tips

  • The word stays hidden until the actor asks for it.
  • The game stays in this browser until someone starts a new one.
